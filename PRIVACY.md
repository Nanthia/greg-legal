# Greg: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Greg is a small, private household assistant run by the GitHub user **Nanthia** (the "operator") for the operator's household. This page explains what information Greg handles and where it goes. It is written in plain language and tries to be honest about the limits.

## What Greg collects

Only what you give it by using it:

- **Your messages to Greg** and the photos you send him, so he can answer.
- **Household information you ask him to keep:** calendar events, grocery and to-do lists, reminders, notes, birthdays and anniversaries, expenses, habits, and saved places.
- **Your location, only if you choose to share it** in Telegram (a pin or live location). Greg keeps only your latest position, overwriting it each time, and does not keep a trail of where you have been.
- **Your account identifiers**: your Telegram and/or Discord numeric user ID, and the name the operator gave you in the household list.
- **Usage counts** (how many calls to the AI service were made and roughly what they cost), so the operator can watch spending.

Greg does not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and does not sell or share information for marketing.

## Where it is stored

On the operator's own computer, in a database file, with a daily backup copy kept on the same computer. Nothing is stored in a cloud database run by Greg. A recent window of each conversation (about the last day, up to a limited number of messages) is kept so Greg can follow what you are talking about; it is trimmed automatically.

## Who else sees it

To do his job, Greg sends information to these services:

- **Anthropic** (the maker of the Claude AI model): your message, any photo you sent, and the parts of the household information needed to answer, such as your notes and lists. Anthropic processes this to generate Greg's replies under its own terms and privacy policy. When Greg searches the web for you, the search is run through Anthropic's service as well.
- **Telegram and Discord:** the messages Greg sends and receives travel through those services, under their own terms and privacy policies.
- **The operator:** because Greg runs on the operator's computer, the operator can technically read everything stored in it. Greg is built so that one person's *private* notes, to-dos, habits and personal spending are never shown to the other person through Greg, but the operator who hosts the database is the exception. Only share with Greg what you are comfortable with the operator being able to see.

Greg has an optional wall-tablet dashboard that shows the household's *shared* calendar, groceries, to-dos and spending on the home network. It is protected by a secret link and is not meant to be reachable from the internet.

## How long it is kept

Until it is deleted. You can ask Greg to delete a note, list item, expense or date at any time, and the `/new` command clears the recent conversation. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis. If you want everything about you removed, ask the operator.

## Children

Greg is not intended for children under 13 (or under the minimum age for the app you use, if higher).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. Using Greg after that means you accept the updated policy.

## Contact

To ask a question or have your information removed, open an issue on this repository or ask the operator directly.
