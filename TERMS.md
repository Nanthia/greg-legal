# Greg: Terms of Service

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Greg is a small, private household assistant bot. These terms explain what it is and what you can expect when you talk to it. They are written in plain language.

## 1. What Greg is

Greg is a personal assistant that helps a household with things like a shared calendar, grocery lists, to-dos, reminders, notes, birthdays and expenses. It runs on the home computer of its operator (the GitHub user **Nanthia**, called "the operator" below) and talks to people through Telegram and Discord.

Greg is **not a public service, not a business and not for sale.** It is not offered to the general public and no one is charged to use it.

## 2. Who can use Greg

Only people the operator has personally added to the household list. Greg ignores everyone else. The bot is not meant to be added to other servers or groups, and the operator may remove anyone's access at any time, for any reason.

## 3. Using Greg properly

Please don't use Greg to harass anyone, to try to break or overload it, to try to read another person's private information, or to do anything illegal. You must also follow the rules of the app you are using (the Telegram Terms of Service and Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines).

## 4. Greg is an AI and can be wrong

Greg's replies are written by an AI model. It can misunderstand you, get a date, time or amount wrong, or record something incorrectly. **Check anything important**, such as appointment times, money amounts and deadlines. Greg is not a doctor, lawyer, accountant or financial adviser, and nothing he says is professional advice.

Reminders and messages are only sent while the operator's computer is on and connected to the internet, so they can arrive late or not at all. Don't rely on Greg for anything where a missed or wrong reminder could cause harm.
